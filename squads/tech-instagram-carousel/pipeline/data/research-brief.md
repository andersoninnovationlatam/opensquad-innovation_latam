# Tech Instagram Carousel Research Brief

## DOMAIN 1: Tech Content Creation for Instagram

**Key Frameworks:**
- Carousel Performance Framework: 8-10 slides optimal, 1080x1440px, carousels achieve 1.92% avg engagement (4x more than Reels)
- Educational Content Structure: Tutorial format, "Things I Wish I Knew", Before/After, Feature breakdown
- Algorithm mechanics: Instagram prioritizes "active dwell time" and "swipe-through velocity", carousels get unique second-chance mechanism

**Output Examples:**
- @tech_insider "8 iPhone Features You Never Knew Existed": 8,900 likes, 2,300 saves
- High-performing types: Step-by-step tutorials, "Things You Didn't Know" lists, Behind-the-scenes tech (5.2% engagement)

**Common Mistakes:**
- Weak opening slide (lose 70-80% in first 2-3 seconds)
- Information overload (walls of text, multiple ideas per slide)
- Inconsistent design (mismatched colors/fonts)
- Over-polished content (2026 algorithm shift penalizes lack of authenticity)
- Generic engagement bait ("Comenta EU", superficial CTAs)

**Quality Benchmarks:**
- Completion rate: 70%+ slides viewed unlocks 3-5x reach to non-followers
- Save rate: 22% higher than single photos
- Caption: First 125 characters critical (feed preview)
- Brasil-specific: 3-5 hashtags optimal (more than 5 can reduce reach in 2026), SEO optimization (Google indexes IG since July 2025)

## DOMAIN 2: Tech Storytelling and Communication

**Key Frameworks:**
- Three-Act Structure: Problem → Shift → Change (22x more memorable than facts alone)
- S.T.O.R.Y. Framework: Structure + Truth + Ownership + Relevance + Yield (3x higher engagement)
- Simplification Framework: Fight "curse of knowledge", use analogies, define at point of use

**Proven Patterns:**
- Transformation Arc: Before → Journey → After with metrics
- Hidden Knowledge: "Things nobody tells you about [tech topic]"
- Simplification: Complex concept → Everyday analogy

**Common Mistakes:**
- Jargon overload (alienates 70-80% who aren't domain experts)
- Feature-focused instead of outcome-focused
- Data dumps without story wrapper
- Ignoring audience's context

**Quality Benchmarks:**
- Clarity test: Would non-technical friend understand?
- Memorability test: Can retell core message after one read?
- Emotional connection: Triggers curiosity, surprise, or recognition?

## DOMAIN 3: Viral Tech Content Patterns

**Hook Framework (First 2-3 seconds):**
- Question hooks: Engage brain's completion instinct
- Shock/statistic hooks: Compelling numbers
- Curiosity gap hooks: Psychological tension
- Pattern interrupt hooks: Visual jolts, increase watch time by 85%
- Contrarian statement hooks: Challenge conventional wisdom
- Educational promise hooks: Clear value proposition upfront

**Edutainment Formula:**
- 66% of users find education + entertainment most engaging
- Short focused lessons (<60 sec per slide)
- Solve real problems + Easy to understand + Worth sharing = Viral potential

**Consistency System:**
- 70% original content, 30% trend-driven
- Dependable schedule (algorithm rewards consistency)
- Repeatable formats (signature series)
- 4.3x higher conversion rates for consistent creators

**2026 Authenticity Shift:**
- Instagram deprioritizes overly polished content (algorithm change January 2026)
- Balance professional design with authentic voice
- Show real examples and outcomes (not generic templates)
- Focus on behavioral transparency and trust signals

**Domain Vocabulary:**
- ALWAYS USE: Framework, acionável, comprovado, passo a passo, insights, resultados reais, case real
- NEVER USE: Revolutionary (sem prova), disruptive (sem contexto), leverage/synergy, "Double tap if...", superlativos sem evidência
